# Final scientific disposition — NYC neighborhood environmental measurement

**Proceed to manuscript writing.** The held-out geography supports estimates of intersectional error and resulting distortions in the measured environment. The prespecified contrasts did not establish a substantial disparity. Further testing on this geography would compromise the prospective design.

## RQ1. What did the systems measure reliably?

The pixel-pooled eight-class macro-F1 values were .774 (ResNet34 U-Net), .817 (SegFormer B0) and .767 (YOLO26n-sem). Mean absolute vegetation-share errors at the tract level were 1.574, .944 and 2.454 percentage points. Constructed-cover errors were 1.661, .970 and 2.511 points. The lowest eight-class compositional error in total variation was the U-Net's 3.029 points. A model's suitability therefore depends on whether the study estimates a particular share or the entire composition. The exploratory frozen I-JEPA readout scored .446 macro-F1. Frozen Qwen produced valid outputs on 2 of 6,314 development windows and no complete development tract. DINOv3 lacked approved weight access. Model comparisons apply to the exact configurations and supervision studied. [Measurement tables](G6J_MEASUREMENT_VALIDITY.md)

## RQ2. How large were intersectional differences in measurement error?

The ecological axes were tract poverty and non-Hispanic Black population share. In the eligible cohort, cells 11, 10, 01 and 00 contained respectively 36, 55, 63 and 111 tracts across 17, 22, 19 and 38 occupied blocks. Blocks can contribute to multiple cells. Across three formal models, both aggregates and both error definitions, the outer limits of the pointwise 95% intervals were −1.93 to +1.92 points for high-high versus low-low and −0.89 to +1.05 for the factorial interaction. In 221 morphology-balanced tracts, corresponding limits were −2.16 to +2.15 and −1.32 to +1.26 (rounded outward). These intervals were narrower than the design magnitudes of 6 and 10 points. No test survived Holm correction across the eight primary contrasts per model and population. The smallest adjusted p was .296 in the 265 and .298 in the 221. Multiplicity-adjusted simultaneous equivalence and resident-level fairness remain unestablished. [Spatial inference and sensitivity](G6J_INTERSECTIONAL_EQUITY.md)

## RQ3. Did measurement change environmental inequality estimates?

The mapped high-high minus low-low vegetation gap was +0.10 points. The U-Net, SegFormer and YOLO estimates were +0.42, +0.45 and +1.03. The corresponding constructed-cover estimates were −0.59, −0.52 and −1.07 versus a mapped −0.14. Mean-gap distortion and the signed RQ2 contrast are algebraically identical. Additional evidence concerns ordering and threshold decisions. A vegetation rank movement of at least ten percentile points affected 1, 1 and 9 of 265 tracts, respectively.

The frozen hypothetical greening screen first applies training-side vegetation and road cutoffs, then caps selections at ten percent of high-poverty tracts (rounded up). Among 91 eligible tracts the cap was ten. The reference yielded five joint candidates and left five slots empty. The models yielded ten, ten and thirteen candidates, respectively, selecting ten each. Relative to the mapped screen they omitted 0, 1 and 0 and added 5, 6 and 5. Five reference selections across five blocks are insufficient for inferential capture comparisons or conclusions about actual allocation.
