# reserve8 spatial protocol

The G6-G freeze prespecified a 3,200-ft cross-split minimum and an extra 800-ft margin for windows and tiles. The tract allocation therefore used 4,000 ft between polygons. G6-H measured the minimum separation after expanding to actual planned input tile envelopes. Both checks predate prospective model outcomes.

| Geometry (ft) | Training–development | Training–evaluation | Development–evaluation | Required |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Tract polygon (rounded) | 4,075 | 4,004 | 4,295 | 4,000 |
| Input tile envelope | 3,317.51 | 3,415.02 | 4,062.19 | 3,200 |

No source JP2 or level-20 delivery tile key is shared across splits. Enlarged input envelopes have positive separation in all three comparisons. This supports fitting on the frozen geography. Nearby block outcomes can still be spatially correlated. Two Brooklyn tracts remain in the original 267-tract target; RGB eligibility limits the observable final cohort to 265.
