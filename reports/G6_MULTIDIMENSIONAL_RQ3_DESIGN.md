# Multidimensional inference on one tract cohort

Primary results are separate equal-tract A−B contrasts in each of eight native area shares (percentage points). For reference p and model q, distortion = mean_A(q−p) − mean_B(q−p). Report observed and estimated contrasts, sign changes, absolute and signed distortion, joint paired block intervals and coverage. Shares sum to one; their signed distortions sum to zero on shared support. Report vegetation, water, roads and buildings separately, with valid-area and resident-weighted sensitivity results.

Hypothetical public-realm greening assessment: within the independently defined high-poverty pool (poverty at or above the eligible-source ACS median), identify tracts with BOTH limited visible vegetation (codes 1+2 ≤ training-reference 25th percentile) and substantial road footprint (code 6 ≥ training-reference 75th percentile). This conjunction identifies low existing green cover where street-space redesign could be investigated. Aerial cover does not establish public ownership, feasible planting, actual need, pollution or that roads harm residents. No substitution between criteria is allowed.

Use equal-tract linear quantiles on the eligible training-geography social pool only. Freeze physical thresholds and empirical midrank CDFs Fv, Fr before evaluation; use identical thresholds and CDFs for reference and AI. On the evaluated social pool of N tracts capacity is ceil(0.10 N); rank joint candidates by descending min(1−Fv(v), Fr(r)), then ascending GEOID. Leave unused slots empty. For N=0, report unavailable. Compare counts, omissions, additions, capture relative to reference selections, unfilled slots and their distribution across social strata. No selection on one criterion alone. Report both marginal eligibility counts, their overlap, vegetation–road rank dependence and the number of tracts for which the second condition changes eligibility. If empirically redundant, report that the joint rule adds no demonstrated dimension.

Prespecified sensitivity: thresholds 20/80 and 30/70; full-city social pool; land-only denominator for coastal settings; ACS stratum uncertainty. Keep the primary rule fixed. Require ≥20 tracts and ≥10 distinct blocks per contrasted social cell. Report selected-set denominators and occupied blocks; do not estimate a subgroup capture rate with zero selections or an inferential interval with fewer than 10 selected blocks. Resample geographic blocks jointly (2,000 draws, seed 614), recompute both lists and capacity per replicate, distinguish repeated tract copies using draw index. Retain fixed source cutoffs. Mark unsupported comparisons explicitly; no causal or funding claims.
