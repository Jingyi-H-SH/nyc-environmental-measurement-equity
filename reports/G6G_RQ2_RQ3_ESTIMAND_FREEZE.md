# Frozen measurement and environmental-justice estimands

The unit is the Census tract. The target is the 267-tract clean frame, with a separately defined common-morphology target. The reference is mapped land cover. No individual attributes, health outcomes or causal exposure effects are observed. The social axes are neighborhood poverty and non-Hispanic Black population share under the inherited strict-frame cuts. Secondary dimensions are older-adult share and rent burden.

**RQ1.** For every tract and each native class, compute predicted minus reference percentage of the identical valid mapped area, including water. Classes are tree canopy, grass/shrub, bare soil, water, buildings, roads, other impervious and rail. In addition report vegetation (tree plus grass/shrub) and constructed cover (buildings, roads, other impervious and rail). Constructed cover is a land-use grouping; the rail class includes corridors of variable permeability. These two aggregates preserve a physical distinction applicable across models. Compute aggregate errors from summed shares before taking absolute values. Report mean signed error, mean absolute error, blockwise variability, complete area, native confusion for dense families, simplex closure and half the sum of absolute native share differences. Opposing native errors can cancel within aggregates. Record model failure separately; calculate paired model contrasts on identical observed support and provide missing-area bounds.

**RQ2, primary.** For each main aggregate, analyze signed and absolute tract share error. Report four equal-tract cell means, 11−00 and 11−10−01+00, in pp. Repeat using the frozen within-cell entropy weights on the common reference morphology. The conditioned estimand equalizes first moments of vegetation and constructed cover. Differences in nonlinear morphology, image availability, geography or reference quality can remain. Retain native-class results as a complete secondary profile, including rare-class support. Report older-adult and rent-burden intersections, centered continuous poverty and racial-share main terms plus product, and leave-one-block deletion as sensitivity. Tracts are ecological units and pixels within them are correlated.

**RQ3, primary.** For each model and reference compute the same environmental inequality gaps and subtract the reference gap from the AI gap, using identical populations and weights. This arithmetic reproduces the corresponding RQ2 signed-error contrast. Count it once as the link from measurement error to substantive inference. Report direction and magnitude for every prespecified surface, with 6 pp for the extreme contrast and 10 pp for the interaction as planning scales. Also report the first Wasserstein distance between AI and reference equal-tract marginal share distributions in each cell, Spearman rank correlation, and fraction changing ≥10 percentile points using midranks in the same cohort. Report AI-minus-reference differences in centered poverty/racial-share association slopes per 10 percentage-point change, conditional on rank and leverage; the product coefficient is scaled per two 10-point changes. No causal interpretation. A hypothetical screening threshold is secondary.

**Uncertainty.** The frame census yields exact descriptive contrasts for observed complete tracts. For uncertainty under geographic replication, report paired block-level covariance using the frozen physical-distance kernel plus spatial sensitivity and a leave-one-block range. For each approximately linear estimator sum weighted, cell-centered residual contributions by block, form the quadratic covariance with the kernel and use the effective-block t multiplier. For ranks/distributions use a delete-one-block jackknife and identify instability when cells disappear. Report within-model pointwise intervals and Holm-adjust comparisons within each model's two aggregates × two error types × two primary contrasts; separate adjusted and unadjusted estimands. Report model contrasts paired on common tracts. Use no significance filter to select surfaces or families. If support or missingness defeats inference, retain descriptive estimates with the reason. Model-based intervals do not include land-cover-reference error, ACS sampling error or unobserved pretraining overlap.
